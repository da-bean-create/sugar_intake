From these sources I was able to get a good idea of the average thought on obesity at the moment. Obesity can cause a variety of diseases/illnesses due to inflammation of the body, hormone imbalance, high blood sugar levels and extreme strain on the body. Some diseases that obesity regularly causes are:<br>
- Chronic inflammation<br>
- Insulin resistance<br>
- Mechanical stress<br>
- Cancer<br>
This provides a decent summary of the effects of obesity and gives an easily readable sign of the thoughts on it (not very good).

However, even though this does not nearly weigh out the downsides, obesity can, in some situations, be benifitial. The excess body fat can help as an insulator and also provides large energy reserves for your body if needed. Furthermore, although cancer is more common with obesity, cancers such as lung cancer, mesothelioma and kidney cancer all have higher survival rates for people with obesity. This shows that obesity has very rare situations where it can be helpful, even though most of those situations are usually caused by the obesity itself.
